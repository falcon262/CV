## Context and the problem

The Ghana Revenue Authority's E-VAT system certifies invoices, and private-sector businesses need their ERP systems connected to it. At PwC Ghana I worked as an engineer on middleware that makes that connection.

Certification is a workflow of several steps, and any step can fail. Whatever happens, an invoice must not be lost or left in a state nobody can explain, and what the middleware records has to agree with what it tells other systems. Certification status also has to be followed up after an invoice is submitted, until the result is known.

## What I did

The middleware is built on four patterns:

- a saga orchestrating the multi-step certification workflow
- a transactional outbox, so records and published events stay consistent
- retries with exponential backoff
- scheduled polling for certification status

It runs on .NET, with Hangfire and SQL Server.

## Outcome

Business ERP systems connect to the Ghana Revenue Authority through the middleware, and invoices are certified automatically and reliably. When a step fails, it is retried or compensated rather than lost.
